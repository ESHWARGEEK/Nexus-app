# POL-SEC-12: Corporate Key Custody & Hardware Security Module (HSM) Policy
Document ID: POL-SEC-12
Classification: Restricted
Allowed Roles: security, leadership
Author: Enterprise Information Security Office

## 1. Scope and Authority
This policy governs cryptographic key generation, custody, rotation, and hardware security module requirements. This policy is RESTRICTED and only accessible to Enterprise Security and Executive Leadership personnel.

## 2. Key Custody and KMS Architecture
All production cryptographic keys used for signing, symmetric data encryption, or tokenization must be hosted in FIPS 140-2 Level 3 certified Cloud Hardware Security Modules (Cloud HSM).
Zero-custody rule: No individual engineer, operator, or administrator shall possess exportable private keys. All key operations must occur over authenticated envelope encryption APIs.

Clause 4.1: Export of root keying material or symmetric key export without dual-custody authorization signed by the CISO and Lead Cryptographer is strictly prohibited and results in immediate automated credential revocation.

## 3. Rotation Schedules
Master keys must automatically rotate every 90 days. Intermediate data encryption keys (DEKs) must rotate every 24 hours or after processing 1,000,000 transactions, whichever occurs first.
